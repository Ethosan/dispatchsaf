# Dispatch: Privacy

Dispatch is a personal project by Ethan Tam. It turns the WPT / MPT vehicle list into copy-paste dispatch blocks, times each WPT, and ticks off completed vehicles. It has no user accounts, no server, and no analytics. This page says what data touches the service and where it goes.

## What is collected

- **Nothing stored about you.** Dispatch doesn't ask for your name, email, or any sign-in. There are no cookies set for tracking.
- **This device.** The app saves your vehicle list, timers, notes, and settings in your browser's local storage so they persist between visits. Anyone who uses that browser can see that copy. Clearing your browser data removes it.
- **Offline cache.** As a PWA, the app caches its own files so it works offline. This is the app's code, not your data.

## Who else processes data

- **Netlify** and **Cloudflare:** hosting and caching the app's static files.
- **Google Fonts:** the page's typefaces load from Google.

## What is not done

- No ads, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cross-site tracking.
- No selling or sharing of personal data (there isn't any to sell).
- No account system — there is nothing to delete because nothing is stored about you server-side.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before it takes effect.
